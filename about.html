LuthuliScents began with a simple belief: smelling expensive should not mean spending a fortune. Every perfume is hand-crafted from scratch, using quality fragrance oils layered to feel complex and refined.

Our scents are inspired by everyday life in South Africa — crisp apple orchards, warm woody evenings and bold florals — and blended to suit every gender and every occasion.
